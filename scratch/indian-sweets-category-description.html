Indian Sweets FAQ

Are your Indian sweets made fresh?

Yes. Quality Sweets makes mithai in-house in Iselin and prepares sweets fresh daily.

Are your sweets vegetarian?

Yes. Quality Sweets is a vegetarian business, and this collection is made for customers looking for vegetarian Indian sweets.

Do your Indian sweets contain preservatives?

Our sweets are made with no preservatives. Because they are freshly prepared, please review each product's availability and fulfillment details before ordering.

Can I order Indian sweets online for shipping?

Many sweets and giftable varieties are available for nationwide shipping. Open the product page to see its current availability and fulfillment options.

Why are some sweets pickup only?

Some delicate products are available only for pickup at the Iselin store so they can be enjoyed in the condition intended by our kitchen.

How can I tell whether an item ships or is pickup only?

Each product page shows its current availability and fulfillment options. If an item is marked for store pickup only, it is not presented as a shipping option.

Can I pick up my sweets at the Iselin store?

Yes. Pickup availability is shown on each eligible product. Quality Sweets is located at 1384 Oak Tree Road in Iselin, New Jersey, and is open daily from 10 AM to 8 PM.

How do I choose the right quantity or size?

Open the product you want to see its available options and select the quantity that fits your order. For a larger gathering, choose your items early so you can review their current availability.

What should I try if I am new to Indian sweets?

Try Bengali sweets such as Rasgulla or Chum Chum for soft, milk-based flavors; choose Kaju Katli or barfi for nutty and creamy options; or start with classic ladoos, pedas, jalebi, and gulab jamun.

Do you have sweets for gifts and celebrations?

Yes. The catalog includes sweet assortments and giftable varieties for sharing, celebrations, and special occasions. Check each product page for its current availability and fulfillment options.

Where can I find ingredient or allergen information?

Ingredients and allergens can vary by sweet. Review the product page and contact the store before ordering if you have an allergy or a specific dietary question.
