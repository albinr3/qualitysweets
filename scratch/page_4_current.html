Frequently Asked Questions About Our Catering

What regions do you cover for B2B catering and delivery?

We provide bulk orders, party platters, and catering services across all of New Jersey (Middlesex, Bergen, Hudson, Somerset), New York (Manhattan, Queens, Brooklyn, Long Island, Westchester), and Connecticut (Stamford, Greenwich).

Are your sweets and food 100% vegetarian?

Yes. Quality Sweets operates a 100% strict vegetarian facility. We use pure milk, fresh chhena, pure ghee, premium nuts, and natural spices with zero animal lard or gelatin.

How far in advance should I place a bulk order or wedding catering request?

For large weddings or live station events, we recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance. For bulk samosas (50–500 pieces) or temple prasad orders, 24 to 48 hours notice is sufficient.

Can I order custom-branded or personalized mithai gift boxes?

Absolutely. We offer custom gold Ganesh boxes, peacock boxes, and custom branded sleeves for corporate Diwali gifts or wedding return gifts.
