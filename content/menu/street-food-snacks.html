Street Food Pickup FAQs

Are samosas available for pickup?

Yes. Samosas are part of the hot-food counter menu; call ahead to confirm availability for your pickup time.

Can I order pakodas by the pound?

Yes. Paneer Pakoda and Cut Mirchy Pakoda are listed by the pound.

Is there dine-in seating?

No. This menu is for counter pickup only.
