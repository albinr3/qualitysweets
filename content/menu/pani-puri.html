2

Frequently Asked Questions: Pani Puri Pickup in Iselin, NJ

Quick answers on kit contents, shelf life, and ordering party boxes.

Will the puris get soggy on the ride home?

No. We never assemble pani puris ahead of time. Puris are boxed completely dry, and waters and fillings are sealed in separate spill-proof containers. They will stay crunchy as long as they remain dry.

Can I eat pani puri inside the store?

No. Quality Sweets operates strictly as a takeout counter and pickup shop with no dining tables. All kits are packed sturdy and cold to enjoy wherever you are heading.

Can pani puri kits be shipped to other states?

No. Prepared street foods and chilled flavored waters cannot survive postal transport. They are sold strictly for counter pickup at 1384 Oak Tree Road. For gift shipments, browse our nationwide sweets collection.

How spicy is the mint water? Can I get only sweet water?

Our classic mint water has a moderate, authentic kick from green chilies and ginger. Every kit also includes sweet date-tamarind water, so you can mix both in each puri to dial in your exact spice preference. If you prefer only sweet water, just ask our counter staff.

How far in advance should I order a 50 or 100-puri party pack?

For party trays of 50 or 100 puris, giving us a call 1 to 2 hours in advance is ideal so we can package fresh waters and filling. For large festival or wedding orders (250+ pieces), please call 24 to 48 hours ahead.

Are the ingredients 100% vegetarian?

Yes. Everything is 100% pure vegetarian, made with potatoes, chickpeas, herbs, spices, semolina, and vegetable oil. No meat, poultry, gelatin, or animal shortening is used in our kitchen.
