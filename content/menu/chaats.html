Counter pickup only — no dine-in and no postal shipping. Freshly prepared at 1384 Oak Tree Rd, Iselin, NJ.

Choose a chaat when you want a sweet, tangy and savory Indian snack prepared for takeout. This Iselin counter menu includes crisp puris, warm chickpeas, yogurt-based chaat and spiced potato dishes. Each item is made for pickup; order ahead if you need a particular selection prepared for your arrival.

Chaat Pickup FAQs

Can I get chaat for dine-in?

No. Chaat is prepared for counter pickup only at the Iselin store.

Should I call before coming in?

Calling ahead is helpful when you want to confirm availability or time your pickup.

Can chaat be shipped?

No. Hot chaat is not available for postal shipping.
