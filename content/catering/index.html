Common Questions

Catering FAQ

What does Quality Sweets provide for catering?

We specialize in freshly fried samosas, handcrafted mithai, bulk savory snacks, custom wedding favor boxes, and live jalebi stations. We focus exclusively on sweets and appetizers (we do not cater full hot lunch or dinner entrees).

How much notice is needed for a bulk order?

We recommend at least 48 hours for standard party trays (50 to 100 samosas), and 1 to 2 weeks for large wedding orders, custom favor boxes, or festival temple batches.

Can I order samosas for a party or office event?

Yes. We offer fixed pricing on 50-samosa ($62.50) and 100-samosa ($120.00) party trays, with mint and tamarind chutneys included. We also offer custom volume pricing for orders of 250 to 1,000+ pieces.

Do you handle temple and mandir prasad orders?

Yes. We regularly supply temples across NJ, NY, and CT with bulk Motichoor Ladoos, Besan Ladoos, and mixed sweets, packaged in food-grade trays for easy distribution.

Do you deliver outside of Iselin?

Yes. In addition to in-store counter pickup at 1384 Oak Tree Road in Iselin, we arrange direct delivery for large orders throughout New Jersey, New York City, and Connecticut.
