Temple Prasad Wholesale FAQs

How do you ensure the purity of temple prasad?

All sweets are made in a 100% vegetarian kitchen using cow ghee, milk khoya, cardamom, and saffron. No onion, garlic, or non-vegetarian items enter our facility.

What is the minimum quantity for wholesale mandir pricing?

Wholesale pricing starts at 25 lbs per sweet variety (e.g. 25 lbs Motichur Ladoo). Discounts increase for orders over 100 lbs, 250 lbs, and 500+ lbs.

Can you provide pre-sealed individual prasad boxes for festival distributions?

Yes. We offer pre-packaged 100g, 250g (1/2 lb), and 500g (1 lb) clear sealed boxes, letting temple volunteers distribute prasad quickly to large crowds.

How far in advance should temple committees order for major festivals like Diwali?

For major festival dates such as Diwali Annakut or Janmashtami, we recommend ordering 2 to 3 weeks in advance to reserve kitchen time and delivery vans.

Do you offer tax-exempt billing for registered 501(c)(3) religious organizations?

Yes. We process tax-exempt invoices for registered 501(c)(3) non-profit temples upon receiving an ST-5 or state tax exemption certificate.
