Wedding Catering FAQs

Can box assortments be customized for dietary preferences?

Yes. All of our sweets are 100% vegetarian. We can customize boxes with nut-free options, dry fruit specials (Kaju Katli), or pure ghee ladoos based on your family's preferences.

How far in advance should we reserve wedding return gift boxes?

We recommend booking wedding favor boxes 3 to 6 weeks before your wedding date to ensure custom box printing and sweet preparation. We can also handle quick turnarounds when needed.

Do you offer tasting sessions for wedding couples and families?

Yes. Wedding families can visit our Iselin, NJ store on Oak Tree Road to sample sweet varieties and view box options.

What is the shelf life of wedding favor mithai boxes?

Dry fruit sweets (like Kaju Katli and Anjeer Roll) stay fresh for 2 to 3 weeks at room temperature. Milk sweets (like Peda and Cham Cham) stay fresh for 5 to 7 days in the refrigerator.

How are wedding return gift boxes delivered to the venue?

We deliver in temperature-controlled vans to venues, hotels, and halls across NJ, NY (NYC, Long Island, Westchester), and CT.
