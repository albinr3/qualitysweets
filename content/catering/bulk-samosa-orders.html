Fresh Samosas Made Daily from 6:00 AM

We make every bulk samosa batch from scratch starting at 6:00 AM in our Iselin kitchen, using classic Punjabi preparation methods:

  • Ajwain Pastry Dough: We roll outer crusts using wheat flour, carom seeds (ajwain), and pure ghee shortening, creating a crisp, non-greasy shell.
  • Punjabi Potato & Pea Filling: Russet potatoes are steam-boiled, cubed, and mixed with green peas, crushed coriander seeds, cumin, dry mango powder (amchur), and fresh green chilies.
  • Fried Fresh for Pickup: Samosas are fried in small batches right before your scheduled pickup or delivery time.

Thermal Packaging & Oven Reheating Instructions

We pack bulk samosas in aluminum hotel pans sealed with double foil lids to hold serving warmth during transit. If your party lasts several hours, follow these steps to reheat:

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Place samosas in a single layer on a baking sheet.
  3. Bake uncovered for 8 to 10 minutes until warm and crisp. (Avoid microwaving, as it softens the crust).

Bulk Samosa Catering FAQs

Are your bulk samosas fried fresh on the day of the event?

All samosas are made fresh daily and fried in small batches right before your pickup or delivery time. We do not use pre-frozen samosas.

How many samosas should I order per guest?

For appetizers or cocktail hour, plan for 1 to 2 samosas per adult guest. For main snack events or office lunches, plan for 2 to 3 samosas per person.

Are chutneys included with bulk samosa orders?

Yes. Every 50-samosa tray includes 32 oz of sweet tamarind chutney and 32 oz of spicy mint chutney. Larger packages receive scaled chutney volumes.

What is the best way to reheat samosas if they cool down during an event?

Preheat an oven to 350°F and bake samosas uncovered for 8 to 10 minutes to make the crust crisp again.

Do you offer Jain samosas without potatoes, onion, or garlic?

Yes. We make Jain Samosas filled with raw banana (kacha kela) upon advance request for minimum orders of 50 pieces.
