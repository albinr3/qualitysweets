🚀 Key Milestones and Growth Highlights (October 2026)

  • Online Sales Reached $2,120.91 (+7,450%): Grew from 1 order ($28.09) in September to 19 completed orders with an average order value of $111.63. This followed redesigning the primary Add to Cart button, removing distractor buttons (Wishlist and Pinterest), and optimizing Batch 1 product detail pages.
  • Google Search Impressions Grew to 24,203 (+59.7%): Google Search Console recorded 769 direct clicks (+212.6%) compared to 246 in September, with click-through rate doubling from 1.62% to 3.18% and average position advancing to #7.3.
  • Peak Customer Activity on Google Business Profile (1,024 Total Actions): The local listing recorded 441 direction requests to the physical store on Oak Tree Rd (+16.1%), 288 phone calls (+20.0%), and 295 direct clicks to the website (+59.5%). These gains followed weekly Google posts and regular uploads of store and product photos.
  • #1 Placements in Google Maps Local 3-Pack: Strong performance across 7x7 scan grids for "indian sweets shop near me" and "bengali sweets" (88% to 92% of scan pins ranking #1 across Edison, Iselin, Woodbridge, and surrounding towns).
  • High-Engagement Organic Traffic: GA4 recorded 928 organic search sessions with an average session duration of 5m 12s and a low bounce rate of 24.89%, showing strong purchase intent from search visitors.
  • Traffic from AI Search Assistants (GEO): Recorded 46 sessions from AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity) following the deployment of /llms.txt and semantic schema markup.
